## 4. Limits

Largest values per variant, against 16,000 symbols (`MAX_CODE_SYMBOLS`), 16,000 URL characters (`MAX_URL_CHARS`) and
QR version 40:

| Variant | Compressions | Largest F len | Largest URL | Largest QR, F at M | Largest QR, F at L | Largest QR, core at M | Every code decodes |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| summary | auto | 404 | 449 | 13 | 12 | 13 | yes |
| summary | raw, deflate, auto | 415 | 460 | 14 | 12 | 13 | yes |
| summary-censored | auto | 404 | 449 | 13 | 12 | 13 | yes |
| summary-censored | raw, deflate, auto | 415 | 460 | 14 | 12 | 13 | yes |
| full | auto | 969 | 1014 | 22 | 18 | 21 | yes |
| full | raw, deflate, auto | 1187 | 1232 | 24 | 21 | 23 | yes |

- **Symbols.** The largest `F` is 415 symbols for the summary variants (2.6 % of 16,000) and 1,187 for the full
  claim (7.4 %), the latter only with raw compression; with `auto` they are 404 and 969.
- **URL.** The largest link is 460 characters for the summary variants and 1,232 for the full claim (7.7 % of 16,000;
  31 % of the 4,000-character warning). No row is above 4,000, so the Share dialog's warning never shows.
- **QR.** With the tail at level M the largest symbol is version 14 for the summary variants (13 with `auto`) and
  version 24 for the full claim (22 with `auto`); at level L it is 12 for the summary variants and 21 for the full
  claim (18 with `auto`), against 40. The capacity ladder never
  reduced anything: in all 54 rows the full code `C.T` was carried at level M with no notice (`noTail`, `lowEc` and
  `none` did not occur), and the core alone fits the same way.
- **What a v3 claim costs in a code.** The cost of the claim alone (the raw core with the title left out), with the
  same puzzles and the claim replaced:

| Request | # | No claim | v2 compact | v3 summary | v3 summary, censored | v3 full | Summary JSON bytes | Summary minus v2 compact |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 26x26-hard | 1 | 235 | 238 | 348 | 374 | 1055 | 69 | 110 |
| 26x26-hard | 2 | 237 | 240 | 350 | 375 | 1061 | 69 | 110 |
| 26x26-hard | 3 | 237 | 240 | 350 | 375 | 1084 | 69 | 110 |
| 26x26-nightmare | 1 | 238 | 240 | 384 | 384 | 1163 | 90 | 144 |
| 26x26-nightmare | 2 | 238 | 241 | 384 | 384 | 1168 | 90 | 143 |
| 26x26-nightmare | 3 | 238 | 241 | 384 | 384 | 1155 | 90 | 143 |

  The summary is 110 symbols longer than the compact v2 form for a hard puzzle, as expected. For nightmare it is
  143 to 144 longer because that claim is `censored` (the summary JSON is 90 bytes instead of 69). A full claim in a
  code would cost 707 to 784 symbols more than the summary (raw); production never writes one.
- **Analytic bounds.** A summary claim is at most 93 bytes of JSON (`{"model":"difficulty-v3","score":99999,
  "level":"nightmare","saturated":false,"censored":true}`), at most about 149 symbols (the task assumed about 95
  bytes and 152 symbols). The full metrics of a v3 claim are bounded by 4,096 bytes of JSON (RES-21), at most 6,554
  symbols: a hand-made code with a claim at that bound would be about 6,800 symbols and URL characters, below the
  16,000 limits but above the 4,000 warning and above what one QR symbol holds with the public prefix (about 3,300
  symbols at level M and 4,200 at level L, measured with `makePuzzleQr`), so the ladder would print neither code nor
  QR. The producers never encode a full claim, so the bound does not apply to any code they make.
