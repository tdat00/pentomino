## 2. The fit

Grid (every combination that satisfies the invariants; the table order is the order of the columns, `base.max` slowest):

| Knob | Values |

- **Hit**: a line is a hit when the v3 level (`levelForScore(score).id`) equals the requested level and, for a requested level index of 3 or more, `Gproved >= 1`. The score is `scoreWithKnobs` of `{ U, deductionTier, G, info, solutions }` of the line (`first.G`, not `Gproved`).
- **Constraint**: on every board of A the median score strictly increases with the requested level index (4 boards: 6x10, 10x10, 12x12 with easy to master, 18x20 with hard to master). The median and the percentiles are nearest-rank values (the lower middle value for an even count), as in the probe record and in the probe tool.
- **Objective**, lexicographic: (0) the fewest boards whose ordering is violated (0 when some set meets the constraint, so that only such sets can win); (1) the largest mean hit rate over the 18 cells of A (each cell weighs the same); (2) the largest smallest cell hit rate; (3) the smallest normalised L1 distance to the starting values (each knob's distance divided by its range width: `base.max` 4, `base.scale` 9, `rule[2]` and `rule[3]` 16, `weights.G` 8, `weights.info` 5.5, `penalty.many` 8); (4) the smallest knob tuple in the table order. Steps (1) to (3) use exact integer arithmetic (hits times 1,200 divided by the cell size, whose common multiple is 1,200; distances in units of 1/1,584), so no tie is decided by floating-point rounding.

1. **The criteria are met inside the ranges.** The proposed set meets the constraint on all four boards of A (section 4) and wins the objective at step (1): 56.46 % against 14.83 % for the starting values, which stand at rank 141,840 of 365,400.
