## 11. Release notes

These are the release notes of the change (C3 (a)); there is no `CHANGELOG.md`. The texts in quotation marks are the visible texts of the
apps (`player/locale.en.js`, `generator/locale.en.js`, `core/browser/print-texts.js`).

### For users

**Hard really needs a real choice (the difficulty scale `difficulty-v3`).** A puzzle's score now follows how a person solves it: forced
placements and deductions first (nine kinds of deduction in three tiers of depth), then real guesses, which are choices whose wrong
alternatives can be proved impossible. A puzzle that needs no guess scores below Hard. The level names and their score ranges are
unchanged (Very easy 0-7, Easy 8-11, Normal 12-16, Hard 17-24, Expert 25-36, Master 37-52, Nightmare 53-76); the score of a given puzzle
differs from before. A puzzle is always rated in one canonical order, so it always gets the same score; a different order of the same
deductions can count a guess more or fewer, and that is a known limitation (design §7.8).

**Levels offered.** The Generator no longer offers the Hell levels. The new rating could not make Hell puzzles reliably on any board size
(a limit of the present search, not of the levels), so Hell 1, 2 and 3 leave the Generator's list and it offers Very easy to Nightmare; the
other levels keep their board sizes. Packs and links made before still open, and their puzzles, Hell ones included, are re-rated for display
when possible. "Require unique solution" is unchanged; a known limitation, in plain words: on large boards a Hard request with it can end with
fewer puzzles than asked (18 x 20 Hard: 2 of 10 in the calibration).

**Generated puzzles.** The Generator aims at the middle of the requested level's score range. Every puzzle it makes at Hard and above has at
least one proved decisive guess. A candidate whose deduction exceeds the solver's limits is not rated and is skipped, and the new ratings never
show the "+" mark. No early stop was built for sets that cannot be rated (a decision of the owner): a request for a piece set whose ratings all
reach their work limits runs to its time limit and ends without a piece set. The same seed makes different puzzles than earlier versions
(algorithm `gen-v3`, policy `mixed-v3`, rating `difficulty-v3`); a seed reproduces a puzzle only with the same versions.

**Estimates.** When the check of a guess's alternatives cannot finish within its budget, the score is an upper estimate: packs mark it with
`censored: true` and the apps show "Decisive estimate: {count} alternatives undecided within the work limit" (or "alternative"), and just "Decisive
estimate" for a rating read from a puzzle code, which carries no count. In the calibration, more than 5 % of the generated puzzles are estimates
on Nightmare on every board size from 3 x 29 to 26 x 26 (15 boards; 34 % to 97 %), on Master on 10 x 10, 15 x 20, 18 x 18, 18 x 20, 20 x 20,
20 x 21, 22 x 22, 24 x 24, 26 x 26 and 32 x 15, and on Expert on 22 x 22, 24 x 24 and 26 x 26; these ship with the note and the flag. The owner
accepted the 17 cells above 25 % (the 15 Nightmare cells, and Master on 24 x 24 and 26 x 26) at the unchanged census budget.

**Puzzles from earlier versions.** A stored rating is never changed. Puzzles rated by earlier versions (`difficulty-v1`, `difficulty-v2`) are
re-rated for display with the new model, with the note "Re-rated with the current scale (original: Easy 8, difficulty-v2)"; when the
re-rating is not possible the stored rating is shown with "Could not be re-rated". Packs, links and puzzle codes made by earlier versions keep
opening (except sets with two pieces of the same shape); version-1 puzzle codes are unchanged; new codes carry a short rating (model, score,
level, the "+" flag and, for an estimate, `censored`), packs the full one. The Player's built-in sample is unchanged: its puzzles keep their
`difficulty-v2` ratings and are shown re-rated with the note.

**Two pieces of the same shape.** A piece set with two pieces of the same shape (mirror images count as the same shape) is refused when a pack,
a link or a puzzle code is opened and when the Generator imports it, with "Pieces {piece} and {first} have the same shape (mirror images count
as the same shape), so this puzzle cannot be opened." (before, the pack reader only warned). A saved Generator set with two pieces of the same
shape is removed when the Generator starts, with a one-time notice in English and Vietnamese: "{count} saved piece sets with two pieces of the
same shape were removed: such sets can no longer be used."

**Generation Steps.** Each puzzle card in the Generator's puzzle list (wide layout) has a "How it was made" button that replays how the puzzle
was made, step by step (the set, its layout, the clue search, the repair, the final rating), with the score table. On a phone (below 768 px)
the list of cards is not shown, so the steps cannot be opened there. The steps exist for puzzles made by this version; for others the dialog
says "Steps not available for this puzzle." Packs written by this version carry a small optional provenance record (`generation.setOrigin`,
`generation.made`) for it. A replay can take a while on a large board and has no time limit of its own: it waits while a generation runs,
closing the dialog stops it, and starting a generation stops it too. One measurement on a 20 x 18 Nightmare puzzle took 1.7 seconds; the
engine's own bound would allow about 20 minutes of work.
