and 16 (`stall16.mjs`; `n16.jsonl`, 76 s) with `rerun.mjs`, and compared with their no-stall baselines. A rerun must
be identical to its baseline up to the first rating at which the rule holds on the baseline sequence (the prefix
property); a call fired by the rule is `same` (the final result is kept), `lost` or `changed`.

TP14 (definition used by `certify.mjs`): a call is **lost** when the baseline result lies in the central half of the
band and the rerun's final result does not (the rerun has a score outside the central half, or no result). A rerun
whose result changed but is still central (another clue set or another central score) is an **other changed
result**, not a lost one; a change of a result that was not central is also an other changed result. The self-test
(`certify.mjs --self-test`, `certify-self-test.txt`; exit 0) prints ten synthetic cases and ends with
"10 of 10 synthetic cases classified as expected":

- a different central puzzle at 21: changed;
- a different central puzzle at 20: changed;
- a loss to 18: lost;
- no result: lost;
- the same result: same;
- a rerun that differs before the firing rating: irreproducible;
- a change of a result that was not central: changed;
- never fires (N = 16), identical rerun: none;
- never fires (N = 16), another result: irreproducible;
- never fires (a central rating), identical rerun: none.
